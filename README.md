# Kodistan (کۆدیستان)

Kodistan is a place for people who work in tech in Kurdistan, and the companies looking for them, to find each other.

## The problem

In Kurdistan there is no good place for developers, designers and other tech people to show their work, share their CV, or find a job. Companies don't know where to look either. Most hiring happens through friends, Facebook groups and pages, or agencies. That works sometimes, but a lot of good people never get seen, and companies miss them.

## What it does

- **Tech people** (developers, designers, testers, data and product people) create a profile, add their CV and show their projects.
- **Companies** post jobs themselves and look for people who fit.
- Everyone finds each other in one place, made for the Kurdish tech community, in Kurdish and English.

## Who it's for

- People working in tech in Kurdistan, and Kurds in tech abroad
- Tech companies and recruiters in Kurdistan

## Built with

React, Node.js and PostgreSQL. Web first, a mobile app later.
